# ZimERP Financial Plan

Status: rough estimates for planning. All figures are USD and must be checked against current prices and real quotes before decisions.

## Startup costs (once-off, before launch)

| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Company registration, trademark search and filing (ZIPO), legal templates | $500–1,500 |
| Domains (`zimerp.co.zw`, `zimerp.com` and variants) | $50–100 |
| Penetration test before launch | $2,000–5,000 |
| Accountant review of tax, payroll and accounting logic | $500–1,500 |
| Brand identity (logo, colours, templates) | $200–1,000 |
| Launch marketing (event, videos, printed material) | $500–1,500 |
| Test devices (Android phones/tablets, Bluetooth printers, scanner) | $300–600 |
| **Total** | **about $4,000–11,000** |

## Monthly running costs

| Item | While building | After launch (first year) |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting (Vercel + Supabase; Azure after launch) | $50–100 | $150–500 |
| AI development tools | $100–200 | $100–200 |
| Other software (error tracking, status page, WhatsApp Business API, email) | $0–50 | $100–250 |
| Marketing ads | $0 | $200–500 |
| Support staff (1–2 people) | $0 | $600–1,500 |
| Field agent and partner commissions | $0 | Paid from revenue |
| Internet, power backup, phone | $50–100 | $100–200 |
| **Total** | **about $200–450** | **about $1,250–3,150** |

## Revenue assumptions

- Average revenue per paying company: about $60/month (mix of Business, Growth and Pro plans, plus add-ons)
- Extra income: setup fees, domains, Microsoft 365 margin, hardware

## Break-even

With monthly costs of about $2,000 after launch, ZimERP breaks even at roughly **35 paying companies** at $60/month, before counting setup fees and resale margins.

| Paying companies | Monthly subscription revenue |
|---|---|
| 35 | ~$2,100 (break-even) |
| 100 | ~$6,000 |
| 250 | ~$15,000 |
| 500 | ~$30,000 |

## Runway

Runway = cash available ÷ monthly costs. Example: $10,000 set aside covers startup costs of ~$6,000 plus about 9 months of building at ~$450/month, or about 2 months after launch at ~$2,000/month if revenue is still zero. Plan to have pilots converting to paid within 2 months of launch.

## Funding options

- **Self-funded** from Bromadex and consulting income (keeps full ownership)
- **Founding-customer prepayments:** annual plans paid up front at launch pricing
- **Grants and competitions** for Zimbabwean and African tech startups
- **Microsoft for Startups** credits for Azure and tools (check current terms)
- **Investors** later, once there are paying customers and growth figures

## Key numbers to track monthly

Monthly recurring revenue, new paying companies, free-to-paid conversion, cost to acquire a customer, churn, average revenue per company, cash in bank, months of runway.
